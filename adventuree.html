Prepare For Your Adventure

Pack smart and stay safe on every trail.

🎒 Equipment Checklist

  • ☑ Comfortable trekking shoes
  • ☑ Backpack and water bottle
  • ☑ First-aid kit
  • ☑ Weather-appropriate clothing
  • ☑ Torch and extra batteries
  • ☑ Snacks and energy bars
  • ☑ Raincoat and sun protection

🛡 Safety Guidelines

  • ✓ Follow your trek guide's instructions.
  • ✓ Stay with your trekking group.
  • ✓ Carry enough drinking water.
  • ✓ Check the weather before departure.
  • ✓ Avoid unknown or unsafe routes.
  • ✓ Keep emergency contacts available.
  • ✓ Leave no litter in nature.
